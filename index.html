About NewHaru

NewHaru is a simple task management app that makes your daily life more productive and meaningful.

Key Features:

  • Intuitive Task Management: Easily add, complete, and delete tasks.
  • Calendar Integration: View and manage tasks by date with a clean calendar view.
  • Recurring Tasks: Easily set up weekly recurring tasks.
  • Achievement Tracking: See your completion rate for each day at a glance.
  • Daily Motivation: Start your day with a new motivational quote every day.
  • Easy Schedule Adjustments: Swipe left to postpone a task to tomorrow, or swipe right to delete it.

NewHaru includes only what you really need, without complicated features. With its clean design and intuitive user experience, anyone can use it easily.

Discover your growth day by day. It's possible with NewHaru.
