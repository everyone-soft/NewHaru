Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2, 2025

Overview

NewHaru ('we', 'our', or 'us') values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the NewHaru app ('App') and how it is used.

Information We Collect

NewHaru collects only minimal information:

  • User-Generated Data: Task items, completion status, repetition settings, and other data you create within the App
  • Device Information: Basic device information for diagnostics in case of app crashes

How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used only for the following purposes:

  • Providing core app functionality (task management, calendar features, etc.)
  • Improving app performance and fixing bugs
  • Enhancing user experience

Information Storage and Security

All user data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers. Data is protected through standard iOS security features.

Sharing Information with Third Parties

NewHaru does not share your personal information with third parties.

Data Retention Period

User data is retained as long as the App is installed on your device. When you remove the App, all local data is deleted.

Your Rights

You have the right to delete the App at any time, which will remove all of your data.

Children's Privacy

NewHaru is not targeted at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will notify you through an in-app notification or email.

Contact Us
